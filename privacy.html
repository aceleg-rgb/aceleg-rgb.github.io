Bertrum privacy policy

Updated November 25, 2026.

Bertrum is a personal assistant used only by its owner on his own computer. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses: with the owner's permission, his own Google Calendar and Gmail, to show his schedule and to label, star and sort his email. It never sends, forwards or deletes email.

Where data goes: data stays on the owner's computer. Short pieces of text may be sent to AI providers to understand requests and sort email. Data is never sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone else.

Google data: Bertrum's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
